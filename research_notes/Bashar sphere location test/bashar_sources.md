# Bashar, the sphere, and the location test

Research cutoff and access date: **10 November 2026, UTC**.

## Scope and access limitations

This is a source-provenance briefing, not a physical experiment. The strongest accessible original-speech leads are not the same as independently authenticated original recordings. During this research, ordinary web search failed and DNS prevented direct access to BasharTV, the third-party transcript hosts, OSF, and YouTube. GitHub's API and indexed code search worked. Consequently, claims about those otherwise inaccessible pages below are **explicitly attributed to earlier public audits**, not represented as newly verified. Project website source files and the outside audit were read directly through GitHub. No original sphere-session audio was played, and no quote or exact sphere-passage timestamp is asserted as verified.

The principal outside source is an [October 21, 2026 provenance audit](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/research/bashar-provenance.md), hereafter **the outside audit**. It is an independent researcher’s secondary source, not a Bashar publisher or a peer-reviewed experimental paper. Its parent snapshot was committed November 10, 2026. A separate [October 16 media audit](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/docs/research/bashar-media-audit.md) describes earlier caption and interview checks. Their reports of what they inspected were not re-performed here.

## Executive findings

- **Attribution:** S.P.H.E.R.E. explicitly credits Bashar material presented by Darryl Anka as inspiration and calls the purported intrinsic locational-frequency variable speculative, not physical evidence. Its selected-resonance test is an operational translation of that inspiration, not a verbatim transcription of the whole claimed effect. [Project whitepaper, v1.0](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/content/whitepaper.mdx)
- **Likely specific source:** The outside audit identifies a third-party transcript attributed to the 2013 event *A Game of Chess*. It reports a thin, hollow copper sphere, approximately 3–4 inches in diameter, tested at two locations on a flat table; the source attributes rolling and possibly disappearance/reappearance to destination-associated excitation. The original audio, event date and transcript fidelity were not independently checked here. [Outside audit](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/research/bashar-provenance.md); [third-party transcript lead](https://www.superphysics.org/research/other/alien/essassani/transmissions/2013/2013-06-01b/)
- **Evidence:** Project documents describe a registered research plan rather than results. A separate outside modeling project explicitly reports **no physical measurements**. Neither an attributed statement nor a simulation is an independent physical replication. [Project protocol source](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/protocol/index.html); [outside model README](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/README.md)
- **Limit:** This search cannot establish that nobody ever tried the experiment or that the claim is true or false. No controlled independent replication report, raw measurement series, or peer-reviewed confirmation or refutation of this specific claim was identified in the sources accessible for this briefing.

## Claim trail and source chronology

### 2013: *A Game of Chess* — closest reported sphere procedure

The outside audit says that the third-party [Superphysics transcript](https://www.superphysics.org/research/other/alien/essassani/transmissions/2013/2013-06-01b/), in its “Gravity and Vibrational Location” section, attributes the following procedure to a June 1, 2013 session:

1. Use a thin, hollow copper sphere roughly 3–4 inches across on a very flat table roughly 6–10 feet long.
2. Excite and measure the sphere at position A and again at B.
3. Excite it at a destination-associated frequency; the reported claim predicts motion toward that location and, separately, possible disappearance and reappearance.

This is **a secondary-source paraphrase of a third-party transcript**, not verified verbatim speech. The audit reports that the transcription does not supply a calibrated observable, a measured effect size, or a dynamical equation. It says the table is described as flat, not frictionless. The [Bashar Archive transcript](https://www.bashararchive.com/transcript/signature-vibration-and-the-vibrational-matrix-map-game-of-chess) reportedly contains substantially similar text but has no verified audiovisual origin or passage timestamp; the two hosts may have copied a common source and must not be counted as independent witnesses. [Outside audit](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/research/bashar-provenance.md)

The outside audit reports that the publisher's [*A Game of Chess* listing](https://tv.bashar.org/programs/game-of-chess) confirms a product title, not the contents of the relevant paid recording. It reports a general Q&A chapter start in the [rental description](https://tv.bashar.org/checkout/new?o=rp3188417), but this **is not a sphere-passage timestamp**. The official title anchor cannot authenticate the wording or the date assigned by the third-party host. Exact title/date, venue, speaker turn and passage should be checked against the original recording or an official transcript before treating them as established. [Outside source register](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/public/data/sources.json)

The audit describes Anka attributing to Bashar the idea that location is a variable of an object's vibration and that changing this variable can lead to relocation without an ordinary intervening trajectory. This is Anka's account of a claim, not an observed transport experiment. The audit says the host chapter for “How reality and space travel work” begins at **01:29:10**, and that it inspected automatic captions around **01:30:10–01:31:40**. These are reported locators, not independently verified quote boundaries; do not cite them as a timestamp for the 2013 sphere experiment. ## Public responses, investigations and evidence

1. **Project S.P.H.E.R.E.** The source of its [protocol page](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/protocol/index.html) states that LOC-1 was registered October 23, 2026 and points to [OSF](https://osf.io/5b6mj/overview); it describes optical measurement of a selected mode, a moved test sphere, a stationary reference, controls and no reported measurement results. The OSF record itself was not accessible here, so registration identity, exact terms, dates and amendment status are **project-reported**, not independently verified in this session. Its proposed four-object variant is stated to be an amendment proposal, not the registered plan. The project separately states that simulation outputs are not physical measurements. [November 3 project note](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/content/research/environment-replay-copper-sphere-relocation.mdx)
2. **Outside investigation:** The public [Location Spectrum Lab](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/README.md), snapshot October 22, 2026, describes an independent simulator and calculations and explicitly says no sphere was physically measured. Its research and mathematical checks are useful leads, not an experimental replication and not proof either way.
3. **Published physics context:** The outside investigation identifies Balakirev et al., “Resonant ultrasound spectroscopy: The essential toolbox,” *Review of Scientific Instruments* 90, 121401 (2019), [DOI 10.1063/1.5123165](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5123165), as a metrology reference about mode measurements and specimen properties. This briefing did not directly read the paper; the outside [source register](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/public/data/sources.json) identifies the inspected manuscript and limits. It is not a test of Bashar's claim.

Missing evidence includes original authenticated audio or an official transcript for the proposed sphere protocol, quantified prediction and measurement operator, calibration/uncertainty and raw A/B data, sham and environmental controls, preregistered decision thresholds linked to the alleged effect, and independently replicated physical results. A repeatable measured shift would still require eliminating mount, thermal, environmental, sensor-geometry, timing and other conventional explanations; it would not by itself establish the proposed mechanism or teleportation. A negative result would constrain only the tested version, conditions and sensitivity; non-significance alone is not equivalence. [Project v1.1 whitepaper](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/content/whitepaper-v1.1-full.md); [outside interpretation](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/research/bashar-provenance.md)

## Interface wording and scientific distinctions

**Suggested source card:** “A claim attributed to Bashar, as presented by Darryl Anka, proposes that an object's location is related to its vibration. A third-party transcript describes a copper-sphere experiment; the original recording and exact wording have not been verified here.”

**Suggested experiment card:** “S.P.H.E.R.E. proposes comparing a selected resonance of a copper sphere at two locations against a reference, while monitoring ordinary environmental and instrumental influences. This is a test of a defined measurement contrast, not a demonstration of unusual transport.”

**Suggested simulator label:** “Synthetic educational data — not measurements or evidence for the claim.” Present flat, shifted, confounded and inconclusive scenarios; don't make a position-induced shift the only possible outcome.

**Physics note:** A sphere can possess multiple mechanical modes. Which response is measured depends on excitation, sensor geometry and boundary conditions; temperature, stresses and the surrounding apparatus can shift or alter apparent resonances. Under a homogeneous environment, a mere coordinate translation of an isolated object does not itself supply a new intrinsic material parameter. “The frequency of a place” or an object's “complete frequency” is not a defined single measurement without a specified observable and method. Ordinary position-dependent environmental effects do not automatically demonstrate an intrinsic location variable. [Project v1.1 whitepaper](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/content/whitepaper-v1.1-full.md); [outside audit](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/research/bashar-provenance.md)

Avoid “Bashar proved,” “frequency encodes coordinates,” “physics predicts all measured resonances are position-independent,” “the experiment replicates teleportation,” and “no one has tested this.” Distinguish an attributed channelled statement, Anka's own account, a testable operational hypothesis, a registered plan, synthetic output, and independent physical evidence. S.P.H.E.R.E.'s [briefing script](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/tools/briefing-script.md) includes source credit and non-endorsement; its broad phrasing that nobody has tested the question should not be repeated as established fact.
