# Bashar sphere location test: primary-source follow-up

Research and access date: **10 November 2026 UTC**. This addendum builds on `official_protocol.md` and `bashar_sources.md`; it does not replace them. The most useful new route is the third-party transcript publisher's public GitHub source repository, which makes the actual attributed passages readable even though its website was unreachable. The OSF and Zenodo records and original recordings remain unverified.

## 1. What do the whitepaper and preregistration actually establish?

### Takeaway

The project's own whitepaper and dated website changes are accessible primary records of **what the project says**, but neither independently establishes the contents or status of the claimed OSF or Zenodo records. A detailed comparison with the actual preregistration is therefore not possible.

### Cited findings

- **Whitepaper, project-authored primary text:** [v1.0 source at the inspected repository snapshot](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/content/whitepaper.mdx) proposes an A/B comparison of a moved copper sphere against a stationary reference. It discusses randomized placements, controls, a two-sided 0.01 primary alpha, and an effect threshold to be fixed after qualification; it describes a research proposal, not measured experimental results. Its front matter and body contain conflicting date metadata, noted in the existing protocol notes. The [site's whitepaper page source](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/whitepaper/index.html) labels the whitepaper version 1.0 and October 22, 2026, and links the DOI. **This is a readable site source, not a verified copy of the archived Zenodo PDF.**
- **Separate draft:** [v1.1 technical draft](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/content/whitepaper-v1.1-full.md) is labelled October 10, 2026 and not operationally adopted. Its normalized frequency-ratio contrast differs mathematically from the formula in v1.0; its metadata template leaves the smallest effect of interest, equivalence alpha and design-effect field null. This comparison concerns **two accessible drafts**, not the OSF registration.
- **Earliest trace found for the record claims:** [October 23, 2026, 03:41:05 UTC commit](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/commit/726dd95fb836331007c50e9ecb9ab41a46196a4f) adds the project's assertions that [OSF 5b6mj](https://osf.io/5b6mj/overview) is a published LOC-1 preregistration and [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23583932](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23583932) identifies an archived whitepaper. The same change still calls the site protocol a draft. [November 4, 2026, 18:55:19 UTC commit](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/commit/1adc5466647d97f65277efbbaf7aca5283f7e61e) changes the README from “draft LOC-1 protocol” to “registered LOC-1 protocol.” These are timestamps and wording in a project-controlled repository, not timestamps supplied or validated by OSF or Zenodo.
- **Claimed difference in role:** the project's [protocol page source](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/protocol/index.html) presents OSF as the controlling preregistered analysis plan and the whitepaper as background and technical context; it says neither reports experimental results. The [four-object measurement page](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/protocol/synchronized-four-object-frequency-measurement/index.html) describes its alternative as proposed, not an adopted amendment. These are **the project's characterizations**, not independent readings of the records.
- **Alternate record checks attempted:** [DataCite DOI API](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.5281/zenodo.23583932), [Crossref works API](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.5281/zenodo.23583932), [Zenodo records API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/23583932), and [OSF public registrations API](https://api.osf.io/v2/registrations/5b6mj/) all failed at DNS resolution with curl; `web_fetch` also failed for DataCite and OSF. Thus there was **no HTTP response and no metadata to interpret**, not a 404 or evidence of nonexistence. Crossref is not necessarily the registration agency for this DOI, so even a future Crossref miss would not alone disprove a DataCite DOI.
- The [GitHub tree API](https://api.github.com/repos/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/git/trees/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338?recursive=1) exposed the site source and whitepaper builder, but the inspected filename search found no PDF or OSF registration export. GitHub indexed searches for `5b6mj` and `23583932` returned project-repository references, not an independently hosted registration or archive. Search-service calls failed with a tool-registration error; no ordinary web-search snippet or cache was obtained. An indexed search hit is not a substitute for reading the artifact.

### Inferences

- The October 23 and November 4 wording changes document a project announcement and subsequent editorial update; they do not prove an OSF registration was actually public on either date, or that its frozen contents match any whitepaper. “Registered” and “draft” may describe different documents or changing editorial language; the commit history alone cannot resolve this.

### Gaps

- OSF record type, creator, submission/publication timestamp, registration template, preregistered variables and exact frozen values, supplements, amendments and accessible files remain unknown. In particular, do **not** fill in the null SESOI or equivalence alpha from a search result or assume v1.0 values are the registration's values.
- DOI resolution, DataCite metadata, Zenodo record/version/creators/publication date, file checksums and whether its PDF matches either GitHub draft remain unverified. A copy of the OSF registration and Zenodo PDF, or later direct access to their APIs, is required for a line-by-line comparison.

## 2. What is the actual Bashar/Anka source and what does it claim?

### Takeaway

We directly read the underlying **third-party** Superphysics transcript source in its GitHub repository. It identifies *A Game of Chess*, attributed June 1, 2013, and a still earlier sphere passage attributed February 28, 1998. Neither transcript's text, event date, title or speaker attribution has been authenticated against the original audio; neither should be called a verified verbatim recording.

### Cited findings

- **2013 source directly inspected:** [publisher's repository, *A Game of Chess*, part 2, “Gravity and Vibrational Location”](https://github.com/jundalisay/superphysics/blob/148e0d6aff8491e1578dab9eb22eb0abf3ddb99f/content/en/research/other/alien/essassani/transmissions/2013/2013-06-01b.md). Its metadata attributes June 1, 2013; the adjoining [part 1 source](https://github.com/jundalisay/superphysics/blob/148e0d6aff8491e1578dab9eb22eb0abf3ddb99f/content/en/research/other/alien/essassani/transmissions/2013/2013-06-01.md) gives the title *A Game of Chess*. In the attributed dialogue, a questioner asks how to measure a proposed locational frequency; “Bashar” suggests comparing the vibration of a thin hollow copper ball, approximately 3–4 inches across, at opposite ends of a very flat 6–10-foot table. The text then predicts that applying the second-position-associated frequency at the first will make it roll toward the second position, or possibly disappear and reappear. This is a proposed outcome in a transcript, **not an account of successful measurement**.
- **Earlier lead directly inspected:** the same publisher's [February 28, 1998 attributed transcript, “Dolphins and Whales” page](https://github.com/jundalisay/superphysics/blob/148e0d6aff8491e1578dab9eb22eb0abf3ddb99f/content/en/research/other/alien/essassani/transmissions/1998/1998-02-28.md) describes measuring a conductive hollow sphere at opposite ends of a roughly 10-foot surface in an isolated chamber, then using an acoustic beam to attempt destination-directed rolling. It suggests 3–6 inches diameter and a maximum 1/16-inch wall, with copper or steel. These values belong to this **1998-attributed transcription**, not to the 2013 passage or LOC-1. “Dolphins and Whales” is the repository page title, not a verified official event title. The file names [YouTube video zVwOsXKRBr8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVwOsXKRBr8); its audio, uploader, coverage of the passage and timestamp were not checked. This is an earlier dated *lead*, not proof that the claim originated in 1998.
- A separate [Chinese-language sphere archive](https://github.com/awakeninglanxin/bashar-sphere-experiment/blob/main/bashar%E7%A7%91%E6%8A%80%E7%B3%BB%E5%88%97/01_%E9%87%91%E5%B1%9E%E7%90%83%E4%BD%8D%E7%BD%AE%E9%A2%91%E7%8E%87%E5%AE%9E%E9%AA%8C_%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8B%E5%87%BA%E5%A4%84%E4%B8%8E%E5%8E%9F%E8%AF%9D.md) also reproduces a 1998-attributed account but cites Superphysics as its source; it is **not independent corroboration** of the recording. Its [README](https://github.com/awakeninglanxin/bashar-sphere-experiment/blob/main/README.md) says its video transcripts include automatic captions not checked line by line, and its GitHub archive contains no original video files.
- **2019 attributed follow-up:** [publisher's source for *Earth Zero*, part 2](https://github.com/jundalisay/superphysics/blob/148e0d6aff8491e1578dab9eb22eb0abf3ddb99f/content/en/research/other/alien/essassani/transmissions/2019/2019-06-22b.md) has a questioner saying they are making a sphere and asking about nickel-copper and frequency. The attributed answer says the room, table and excitation apparatus affect the frequency and declines to specify a universal range. A questioner's claim of construction is **not a completed independent measurement**. This transcript represents a quarter-millimeter figure, but the [outside audit](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/research/bashar-provenance.md) warns that automated audio wording is ambiguous, so do not adopt the dimension without original-audio verification.
- **2023 attributed follow-up:** [publisher's source for *Ringing the Bell*, part 3](https://github.com/jundalisay/superphysics/blob/148e0d6aff8491e1578dab9eb22eb0abf3ddb99f/content/en/research/other/alien/essassani/transmissions/2023/2023-09-10c.md) presents a question about frequency sweeps and the answer returns to measuring the sphere at both locations and their difference, distinguishing rolling from teleportation. Numerical speculation from the questioner is not a validated operating parameter.
- **Official-source boundary:** the earlier [independent provenance audit](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/research/bashar-provenance.md), dated October 21, 2026, reports an [official *A Game of Chess* listing](https://tv.bashar.org/programs/game-of-chess), and says a rental listing gives Q&A starting at 01:00:23. That is a general chapter locator, **not** a sphere-passage timestamp. It also identifies an [official *Ringing the Bell* listing](https://tv.bashar.org/programs/ringing-the-bell). Neither official listing nor recording could be reached in this follow-up; no sphere-passage timestamp or speech was independently verified from audio. The audit reports a separate 2019 third-party transcript's 04:32–06:21 as a relevant locator, but could not retrieve the linked audio; do not treat it as verified recording evidence.
- The project's [v1.0 whitepaper](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/content/whitepaper.mdx) credits Bashar material presented by Darryl Anka as inspiration and does not identify a particular recording. The 230 kHz criterion appears in project material; none of the passages inspected above establishes it as an authenticated original instruction.

### Inferences

- LOC-1's stationary-reference, randomized two-position resonance comparison addresses a limited operational question inspired by the attributed story. It does not implement the claimed destination-directed rolling or teleportation test; a difference in a resonance measurement, even if reproducible, would not by itself establish either outcome or mechanism.
- The third-party GitHub files let us verify that the transcriber **published these words**, not that Anka actually spoke them, when he spoke them, or that their technical specifications are correct.

### Gaps

- Original recording, verified event date, full context, exact sphere-passage timestamp, and any publisher-authorized transcript remain unavailable. Official video listings were not directly verified in this pass. Avoid attributing precise quotations to Anka or Bashar until the original is checked; this addendum paraphrases the third-party transcripts.

## 3. Is there credible independent experimental replication?

### Takeaway

No controlled, independently documented physical replication of this specific claim was identified in the accessible sources. That is a bounded search result, not a claim that no one has ever attempted it or that a negative experiment has been demonstrated.

### Cited findings

- The project itself reports no experimental measurement results on its [protocol page source](https://github.com/cssofny2/spheredesci.org/blob/3a651256e92f5a674e34f44e8756f30cd1caf338/protocol/index.html). Its simulator and proposed future studies are not physical evidence.
- The separate [Location Spectrum Lab README](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/README.md) and its [provenance audit](https://github.com/occult-kranti/brainwave_opensync/blob/336e3fe74e4513b314333b565455f0afa85eca1c/location-spectrum/research/bashar-provenance.md) describe independent analysis/modeling and explicitly report no physical sphere measurements. It is not a replication.
- The newly found [Chinese-language archive](https://github.com/awakeninglanxin/bashar-sphere-experiment) describes source collection and proposed apparatus, not a documented controlled dataset or independent positive replication in the material inspected. The 2019 transcript's questioner reports construction intent, not outcomes or a usable experimental record.
- GitHub repository searches for `bashar sphere`, `bashar transcript`, and `location spectrum`, plus indexed code searches for Bashar/copper/sphere and Bashar/sphere/experiment, were conducted on November 10. Results included the archive and modeling work above, with substantial irrelevant results; indexed search coverage and ranking are incomplete. General web search and scientific metadata APIs were unavailable, so no systematic literature review was possible.

### Gaps

- No accessible report identified a laboratory, preregistered analysis, raw A/B data, calibration and uncertainties, sham/environmental controls, and independent reproduction of either a residual position-associated resonance or destination-directed motion. Peer-reviewed databases could not be searched independently in this environment. Ordinary resonance-metrology research is relevant context but is not a replication of the special location claim.

**Future simulator evidence label:** project-authored proposal / third-party attributed transcript / independent synthetic model. Do not present any as observed experimental evidence. Preserve separate scenarios for null, confounded, inconclusive and hypothetical signals.
