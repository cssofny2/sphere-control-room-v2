S.P.H.E.R.E. Metrology Lab

The S.P.H.E.R.E. Metrology Lab is an interactive, educational simulator of the measurement laboratory proposed by Project S.P.H.E.R.E. (Spatial Positioning Harmonic Empirical Resonance Experiment). It lets you explore how a copper sphere resonance measurement could be set up, run, checked and troubleshot.

This is a simulation for learning and review. It does not produce measurements, datasets or scientific validation, and nothing in it is a research result. The project is at proposal stage.

What you can do in the lab

  • Follow a training mode with an interactive checklist for a location-variable measurement.
  • Work through challenge mode, where simulated faults must be found and corrected.
  • Watch an operator dashboard and adjust an environmental chamber and XYZ stage.
  • Inspect simulated instrument channels such as the RF monitor, scaling, offset and coupling.

Requires JavaScript

The lab runs in your browser and needs JavaScript. If you can read this text, the interactive lab has not loaded yet, or scripts are disabled in your browser.
