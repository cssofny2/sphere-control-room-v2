## Q factor
Keywords: q, q factor, quality factor, peak width, sharp
The Q factor describes how sharp a resonance is: centre frequency divided by peak width. Lower pressure and cleaner conditions raise Q. The challenge target is above 4000.

## Smith chart and S11 dip
Keywords: smith chart, s11 dip, resonance dip, reflection
S11 is the reflected power fraction. At resonance the sphere absorbs power so S11 dips sharply. The dip position is the resonant frequency.

## Interferometry
Keywords: interferometry, doppler, interferometer, optical
The LDV compares the outgoing laser with the light reflected from the vibrating surface. The Doppler shift gives surface velocity without touching the sphere.

## Reference plane
Keywords: reference plane, calibration plane, solt, open short load
SOLT calibration measures short, open, load and through standards so the VNA can remove cable and fixture errors and place the measurement at the probe.

## Mass loading and air damping
Keywords: air damping, mass loading, why vacuum, why vacuum chamber
Air adds mass and damping to a vibrating sphere, which shifts and broadens the resonance. Removing the air narrows the peak and removes pressure as a variable.

## Why isolation and shielding
Keywords: why shielding, why isolation, noise sources, why faraday
Vibration isolation stops floor motion (footsteps, HVAC, seismic) from entering the LDV signal. The Faraday cage blocks radio and mains interference from entering the RF measurement.
